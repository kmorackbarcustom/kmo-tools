# KMO WORK RULES — DRAFT V1
Date: 2026-10-06
Status: DRAFT / LEGAL HOLD ON WORKING HOURS

## 1. Scope
These rules apply to KMO Rack Bar Custom employees. Current workforce baseline: 5 Thai employees, age 18+, technician/mechanic roles. ADMIN users are the shop owner and system admin; ADMIN users are not treated as employees for attendance/leave in KMO Tools.

## 2. Employment
- Employment is indefinite-term unless a lawful written agreement states otherwise.
- KMO probation policy: 119 days.
- Probation does not remove statutory employee rights.
- Existing employees should sign current-date employment documents; no backdating.

## 3. Working schedule — DRAFT OPERATIONAL SETTING
- Normal shop schedule: 09:00–18:00.
- Total break: at least 1 hour/day.
- Weekly holiday: Tuesday.
- Break timing may be flexible but must remain compliant with statutory continuous-work/break requirements.
- LEGAL HOLD: metal welding is identified by Ministry of Labour guidance as hazardous work with a stricter normal-hours limit. This schedule is recorded as KMO's current operational draft only and is not legally cleared for final publication/enforcement until the hazardous-work hours issue is resolved.

## 4. Attendance
- Employees clock in/out through the approved KMO system.
- Attendance requires shop geofence validation.
- Official start time remains 09:00 while this draft schedule is in use.
- Grace period: up to 30 minutes after scheduled start. Grace is KMO leniency and does not change official start time.
- After grace -> LATE.
- No clock-in and no valid leave by workday close -> ABSENT pending review.
- Missing clock-out -> MISSING_CLOCK_OUT.
- Clock-out before scheduled end -> EARLY_LEAVE.
- Employee cannot directly edit attendance records.
- Correction requests must be submitted by the next working day where reasonably possible and require ADMIN approval.
- Original records and all ADMIN changes must remain auditable.
- No automatic fine or wage deduction from attendance status.

## 5. Overtime
- OT requires Owner instruction or approval.
- OT is paid hourly at not less than the statutory minimum rate.
- A late clock-out is not automatically approved OT.
- Late clock-out -> OT_REVIEW. ADMIN determines whether actual work occurred and records approved time.
- LEGAL HOLD: do not enable hazardous-work OT/holiday-work automation until legal limits are confirmed.

## 6. Weekly-holiday work
- Tuesday remains the weekly holiday.
- Exceptional work on Tuesday must be specifically instructed/approved by Owner and recorded separately.
- Do not retroactively convert Tuesday into a normal workday.
- Holiday work and any holiday OT must comply with statutory restrictions and pay rules.

## 7. Holidays
- KMO provides at least the statutory minimum number of traditional holidays each year, including National Labour Day.
- KMO normally includes New Year and Songkran among annual holidays.
- Annual holiday calendar must be announced in advance and cannot be published below the legal minimum.

## 8. Leave
KMO provides at least statutory minimum rights. System/legal minimums cannot be reduced by ADMIN.

### 8.1 Sick leave
- Sick leave is for actual illness.
- Paid sick leave is provided up to the statutory paid limit.
- Where legally permitted, KMO may request a qualifying medical certificate for 3 consecutive working days or more.
- Repeated short sick leave may be reviewed for facts but is not automatic misconduct.

### 8.2 Necessary-business leave
- Provided at least at statutory minimum.
- Planned leave should be submitted at least 1 day in advance when reasonably known.
- Emergency cases may be notified as soon as reasonably possible.

### 8.3 Annual vacation
- Provided at statutory minimum.
- After 1 continuous year, at least 6 working days/year.
- Carry-over is not automatic unless law/agreement/policy provides it.

### 8.4 Maternity/family leave
Use the current Labour Protection Act baseline, including the 2025/2568 amendment effective 7 Dec 2025:
- maternity leave up to 120 days per pregnancy;
- employer-paid maternity leave up to 60 days;
- additional qualifying child-care leave and spouse leave as provided by current law.
System entitlement values must be versioned to the effective law.

## 9. Wages and benefits
- Employees are monthly paid; normal payday is month-end.
- KMO Tools HR V1 does not store salary amounts and is not a payroll engine.
- Confirmed KMO benefit: lunch is provided.
- Reported benefits requiring exact-policy wording before publication: accommodation for some out-of-province staff; performance-based year-end bonus.
- Benefits must not be represented as guaranteed fixed entitlement unless Owner intentionally approves that wording.
- No automatic wage deductions or disciplinary fines.

## 10. Discipline
General process:
1. record incident/facts;
2. coaching/verbal or recorded warning as appropriate;
3. written warning where appropriate;
4. further lawful disciplinary action based on facts, severity and law.
Serious misconduct may be handled under applicable law without every progressive step where legally permitted.
No automatic dismissal based solely on an attendance status or allegation.

## 11. Damage to customer/KMO property
- Record incident and preserve evidence.
- Determine accident, work error, negligence or intentional conduct.
- KMO handles customer-facing resolution as appropriate.
- Employee liability or wage deduction is never automatic and must satisfy applicable law and procedure.
- HR V1 contains no deduction engine.

## 12. Safety and PPE
Required task-appropriate PPE must be used.
Confirmed available PPE includes welding mask, safety glasses, gloves, paint-vapour respirator, safety shoes and hearing protection.
- Unsafe hazardous work must stop when required PPE is not worn.
- Repeated refusal may enter disciplinary review after facts are recorded.
- No personal phone use/headphones while actively performing hazardous tasks.
- No sandals, unsafe loose clothing or jewellery around machinery.
- Long hair must be secured where entanglement risk exists.
- No working while intoxicated; illegal drugs are prohibited.
- Smoking/vaping is prohibited in work, gas, flammable and other hazardous areas.

## 13. Hazardous-task scope
Includes gas welding, grinding, cutting steel, drilling, press/bending machine, painting, motorcycle lifting/jacking and tool-based accessory installation.
Safety training must be documented. Current informal-only training is not treated as complete compliance.

## 14. Customer motorcycles
- No road test riding as part of normal KMO work.
- Customer motorcycles/property may only be handled for authorized work purposes.
- No personal use.

## 15. Personal work
Personal/friends' motorcycles may be worked on at KMO only with Owner approval, subject to case-by-case agreement on materials/space/cost and without disrupting KMO work or safety.

## 16. Customer diversion
Employees may not divert KMO customers, jobs, orders or business opportunities to personal payment/work without Owner authorization.

## 17. Data and confidentiality
Customer data and genuine non-public KMO business/trade information may only be used for authorized work.
No unauthorized copying, forwarding, retention, disclosure or personal use.

## 18. Content/social media
Employees may create personal content and earn personal channel income using KMO space if work, safety, customer privacy and KMO confidential information are protected.
Official-looking use of KMO branding or sponsorship relying on KMO brand requires Owner approval.

## 19. KMO tools/property
No KMO tool/material/part/equipment/property may leave the premises without Owner permission.

## 20. CCTV
KMO uses CCTV and has informed employees / posted signs. Final privacy compliance must confirm purpose, access, retention and rights channel.

## 21. Resignation/offboarding
- Resignation notice must comply with current law and contract/pay-cycle rules.
- Handover includes pending work, customer motorcycles, parts/materials, tools/property, keys/equipment and continuity information.
- System access must be revoked at offboarding.
- Wages must not be unlawfully withheld merely because handover is incomplete.

## 22. Social Security
Status is HOLD / VERIFY.
Some workers may currently be under Section 39 after prior employment ended. Do not treat Section 39 as a substitute for employer Section 33 duties if KMO is now a covered employer and the persons are current employees.
